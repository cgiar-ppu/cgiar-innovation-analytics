## Megatrends

The following megatrends are used in CGIAR foresight research and portfolio planning (from the Innovation Portfolio Management tool and Foresight Initiative):

1. **Climate change and environmental degradation** -- Rising temperatures, extreme weather, resource depletion
2. **Population growth and demographic shifts** -- Urbanization, aging populations, youth bulges
3. **Diet and nutrition transition** -- Shift toward processed foods, rising obesity alongside malnutrition
4. **Digital transformation and automation** -- AI, precision agriculture, digital marketplaces, data-driven farming
5. **Geopolitical instability and conflict** -- Trade disruptions, migration, fragility affecting food systems
6. **Income inequality and poverty persistence** -- Growing wealth gaps, rural-urban disparities
7. **Land use change and biodiversity loss** -- Deforestation, habitat loss, genetic erosion
8. **Water scarcity and competition** -- Increasing demand, declining availability, cross-sector competition
9. **Energy transition** -- Shift to renewables, bioenergy, impacts on agricultural systems
10. **Changing governance and policy landscapes** -- Decentralization, multi-stakeholder governance, evolving trade agreements

These megatrends inform scenario planning and portfolio prioritization decisions.
