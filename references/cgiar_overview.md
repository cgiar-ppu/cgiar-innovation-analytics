# CGIAR Organizational Overview

## What is CGIAR?

CGIAR is the world's largest publicly funded agricultural research partnership, dedicated to reducing poverty, enhancing food and nutrition security, and improving natural resources and ecosystem services. Originally founded in 1971 as the Consultative Group on International Agricultural Research, CGIAR operates through 15 research centers and hundreds of partner organizations across the Global South.

CGIAR's mission centers on transforming food, land, and water systems in a climate crisis. Its research portfolio is organized around five Impact Areas:

1. **Nutrition, Health and Food Security** -- End hunger and enable affordable healthy diets
2. **Poverty Reduction, Livelihoods and Jobs** -- Lift rural populations above the extreme poverty line
3. **Gender Equality, Youth and Social Inclusion** -- Close gender gaps in economic resources and opportunities
4. **Climate Adaptation and Mitigation** -- Implement national adaptation plans and reduce agricultural emissions
5. **Environmental Health and Biodiversity** -- Stay within planetary environmental boundaries

### Initiatives (INIT-01 to INIT-35)

Under the 2022-2024 portfolio, CGIAR organized its work into 35 research Initiatives. Each Initiative is a time-bound, results-oriented project that maps to one or more Science Programmes. Key examples include Accelerated Breeding (INIT-01), Seed Equal (INIT-06), Excellence in Agronomy (INIT-11), ClimBeR (INIT-23), and Digital Innovation (INIT-25). Initiatives are the primary organizational unit for reporting results in PRMS.

### Action Areas

CGIAR's work falls under three strategic Action Areas:

1. **Systems Transformation** -- Food systems driving sustainable land/water use, planetary boundaries, livelihoods, gender equality, healthy diets, climate risk management
2. **Resilient Agrifood Systems** -- Farming and food supply for healthy/safe diets, decent livelihoods, gender equality, youth inclusion, climate solutions, environmental stewardship
3. **Genetic Innovation** -- Genebanks, crop breeding, and seed systems for poverty reduction, gender equality, nutrition, climate, and environment

## The Portfolio Performance Unit (PPU)

The PPU is the unit within CGIAR responsible for monitoring and managing the performance of the research portfolio. The PPU:

- Oversees result reporting across all Initiatives and Science Programmes via PRMS
- Tracks innovation development, scaling readiness, knowledge products, capacity sharing, and policy changes
- Provides portfolio-level analytics to senior leadership, funders, and programme managers
- Manages the Annual Technical Report (ATR) process
- Champions data quality and evidence-based decision making

Key PPU stakeholders include portfolio managers, programme leaders, accelerator leads, and external funders.

## PRMS: Performance and Results Management System

PRMS is CGIAR's central database for tracking research outputs, outcomes, and impacts. It is the authoritative system of record for:

- **Innovation developments** (5,236 in database) -- new technologies, practices, policies being developed
- **Knowledge products** (13,800) -- publications, datasets, reports
- **Capacity sharing** (4,844) -- training events, capacity development activities
- **Innovation use** (1,228) -- adoption and uptake of innovations by users
- **Policy changes** (682) -- policy influence and enactment
- **Innovation packages** (255) -- bundles of complementary innovations for scaling
- **Other outputs and outcomes** (5,289) -- additional tracked results

Total: approximately 32,005 results tracked across all types.

PRMS uses CLARISA (CGIAR's reference data system) as its master data source for controlled vocabularies: countries, regions, institutions, innovation readiness levels, SDG targets, and other reference lists.

### Data Structure

Results in PRMS are linked to:
- **Initiatives/Programmes** via the Theory of Change (TOC) mapping
- **Geographic scope** (global, regional, multi-national, national, sub-national)
- **Partners** (CGIAR centers, external institutions, delivery partners)
- **Evidence** (URLs to supporting documents, publications)
- **Bilateral projects** (funding sources: W3 pooled or bilateral restricted)
- **SDG targets** and **Impact Area indicators**

### Funding Mechanisms

CGIAR funding flows through three primary channels:
- **Window 1 (W1)**: Pooled, unrestricted funding allocated by the CGIAR System Council
- **Window 2 (W2)**: Pooled funding allocated to specific Initiatives/CRPs
- **Window 3 (W3)**: Project-specific grants from donors with restricted scope
- **Bilateral**: Direct funding agreements between donors and individual centers

## CG Insights Ecosystem

CG Insights is the planned centralized "front door" for CGIAR's AI-powered analytical products. It envisions a unified platform where programme leaders, funders, and partners can access multiple analytical tools through a single interface. The Innovation Analytics Platform is one of several products being developed under this vision. Integration with CG Insights will involve shared authentication, consistent branding, and API-level interoperability.
